# Project Brief

## Project

E-commerce Product Analytics Case Study

## Project type

Independent portfolio product analytics project based on a real/open e-commerce dataset.

## Main objective

Conduct an end-to-end product analytics investigation that resembles the work of a product analyst:

data understanding → business problem → metrics → investigation → analytical hypotheses → validation → product hypotheses → experiment design → dashboard → recommendations.

The project should demonstrate:

- product thinking;
- SQL;
- Python;
- exploratory analysis;
- metric design;
- statistics;
- experimentation;
- visualization;
- communication of analytical findings.

# Deliverables

The completed project should contain:

1. Reproducible data preparation.
2. Data-quality audit.
3. Data dictionary.
4. Exploratory analysis.
5. Clearly defined business problem.
6. Metric framework.
7. Deep investigation of the selected problem.
8. Analytical hypotheses and their validation.
9. Statistical analysis where appropriate.
10. Product hypotheses.
11. A/B experiment design for the strongest product hypothesis.
12. Dashboard with meaningful product KPIs.
13. Final analytical report.
14. GitHub README.
15. Interview defense notes.

---

# Constraints

The dataset is observational unless explicitly documented otherwise.

Do not claim causality from observational relationships.

Do not invent unavailable product or business information.

Clearly distinguish:

- facts contained in the dataset;
- calculated results;
- assumptions;
- interpretations;
- hypotheses.

---

# Definition of success

The project is successful if another analyst can:

1. reproduce the calculations;
2. understand why each important analytical decision was made;
3. identify the limitations;
4. follow the evidence from business question to recommendation;

and if the project owner can independently defend all major decisions during an analyst interview.
